Payment settings

Which currencies and amounts a mini app may charge (policy only until payments are built).

Overview

Payment settings say which currencies a mini app may charge in, whether it may place a hold (reserve money now, charge later), and the smallest and largest single amount it may ask for.

Policy only for now

The payment feature itself (the pay.request capability and the flow that asks a user to pay) is not built yet. These settings are stored, audited and shown in the backoffice so the policy is already in place when payments ship, but no service enforces them today. Do not promise partners that a limit set here stops anything yet.

Why this existsMoney limits belong to the platform, not to the partner: a small pilot partner should be able to charge only in ETB and only between 10 and 5,000, and only a trusted one may use holds. Putting these rules next to the capability decision means they are reviewed by the same people.

Fields

FieldTypeRequiredRulesWhy
allowedCurrencieslist of stringsyesEach ETB or USD (any letter case, stored upper case, duplicates removed). An empty list means no currency.A partner charging in the wrong currency is a support problem.
allowHoldbooleannotrue allows authorise-now-capture-later; anything else, or absent, is false.Holds tie up the user's money; enable them deliberately.
minAmountMinorinteger ≥ 0 or nullnoIn minor units (cents or santim): 1000 = 10.00. Absent or null = no minimum.Avoid dust payments.
maxAmountMinorinteger ≥ 0 or nullnoSame units. Absent or null = no maximum. minAmountMinor must not exceed it.Cap on a single payment.
Watch out

The PUT replaces all four fields. Leaving out minAmountMinor or maxAmountMinor clears it, and leaving out allowHold turns holds off. Read the current values first (payment in GET /admin/api/v1/apps/{appId}; it is null until settings were saved once).

Endpoint

PUT/admin/api/v1/apps/{appId}/payment-settingsSet payment settings
Who may call
ADMIN or SUPER_ADMIN
Auth
Staff session Bearer token at assurance level aal2 (Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN)
Rate limit
300 requests per minute per client IP at the gateway (HTTP 429 beyond that)
Backoffice
App > Payment (Allowed currencies, "Allow holds", Minimum and Maximum amount in minor units; a note says it is policy only)
Audit name
payment.set (meta: currencies, allowHold)
Reversible
Yes: send the previous values

Purpose. Creates or replaces the payment settings of one app. When to use it. When you grant pay.request to a partner, or change what it may charge.

curl -sS -X PUT "$GATEWAY/admin/api/v1/apps/$APP_ID/payment-settings" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
  "allowedCurrencies": [
    "etb",
    "USD",
    "ETB"
  ],
  "allowHold": true,
  "minAmountMinor": 1000,
  "maxAmountMinor": 5000000
}'

Response (HTTP 200)

The saved settings (currencies normalised):

HTTP 200 OK

{
  "allowedCurrencies": [
    "ETB",
    "USD"
  ],
  "allowHold": true,
  "minAmountMinor": 1000,
  "maxAmountMinor": 5000000
}

Effects, audit

  • Audit: payment.set, meta {"currencies": [...], "allowHold": true} (the amounts are not in the event).
  • No other service reads these settings yet (see the box above). Reversible: send the previous values.
  • Setting payment settings does not grant pay.request: that is a separate decision on the Capabilities page.

Scenario

A ticketing partner is approved to take payments. You grant pay.request with the reason "To pay for tickets you choose", then set ETB only, min 1000 (10.00), max 5,000,000 (50,000.00), holds off.

Not built yet

  • The payment flow itself (pay.request): nothing reads or enforces these settings yet.
  • Currencies other than ETB and USD.
  • Clearing the settings (there is no delete; send an empty currency list).
