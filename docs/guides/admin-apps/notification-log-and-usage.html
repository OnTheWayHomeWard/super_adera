Notification log and usage

What a mini app sent, what happened to each message, usage against the limits, and how to answer "why did it not arrive?".

Overview

Four read-only views, all under /admin/api/v1/notification/, show what a mini app has sent through the partner API: a searchable log, one notification with every delivery attempt and callback, counts, and usage against the limits. Any staff role may read them. They never show the message values, user ids or aliases, because those can contain personal data.

Why this existsWhen a partner says "your platform did not deliver my message" you must be able to answer in a minute, with facts: was it accepted, what was decided, which provider said what, did we report back. These views are that evidence.
Note

These calls are handled by the notification service behind the same gateway route and the same staff check as the rest of the admin API (rate limit 300 per minute per IP, aal2 token). Times are UTC.

Statuses and reasons

StatusMeaningFinal?
QUEUEDAccepted and waiting for the delivery worker.no
PROCESSINGA worker is delivering it right now.no
RETRYINGA temporary problem (provider timeout, a dependency down). Will try again; reason says why and nextAttemptAt when.no
SENTHanded to the provider (SMS, email) or stored in the user's inbox (in-app). For SMS and email this means the provider accepted it, not that the phone received it.yes
FAILEDGave up: the provider rejected it permanently (bad number or address), or 8 attempts were used.yes
UNREACHABLEThe user cannot be reached: account deleted or suspended, or no phone or email on file for the chosen channel.yes
SUPPRESSEDDeliberately not sent: the user muted this app on that channel, or the live allowlist excluded the recipient.yes
EXPIREDKept trying until the message's time-to-live ran out (default 24 hours, partner can ask for 1 minute to 7 days).yes
reasonSeen withMeaning
recipient_opted_outSUPPRESSEDThe user turned off this app's notifications for this channel (done at acceptance or just before delivery). Nothing to fix; it is the user's choice.
not_allowlistedSUPPRESSEDReal providers are in use and a "live allowlist" (a safety list used during rollout) does not include this recipient.
user_unreachableUNREACHABLEThe user was deleted or suspended between acceptance and delivery.
no_phone, no_emailUNREACHABLEThe user has no phone or email for the chosen channel any more.
twilio_http_NNN, resend_http_NNNFAILED, RETRYINGThe SMS or email provider answered HTTP NNN. 4xx other than 408 and 429 are permanent (FAILED at once); 5xx, 408, 429 and timeouts are retried.
max_attempts:...FAILEDAll 8 delivery attempts were used; the text after the colon is the last problem.
user_directory_unavailableRETRYINGAdera's own user service could not be reached; retried.
expired (or the last problem)EXPIREDThe time-to-live ran out before a successful delivery.

Retries: after a failed attempt the next one is scheduled after 30 s, 1 min, 2 min, 4 min, ... at most 1 hour apart (give or take 10 percent), up to 8 attempts or the message's expiry, whichever comes first.

Attempts, callbacks

ItemValues and meaning
attemptLog[].outcomeOK (provider accepted), SKIPPED (not tried, e.g. no_devices: the user has no registered device so no push; the inbox entry was still created; or not_allowlisted), TRANSIENT (temporary failure, will retry), PERMANENT (will not retry).
attemptLog[].providerconsole (dev SMS), mailpit (dev email), twilio, resend, the push provider (fcm, or the dev fake), or sandbox for the u_p_test_* test users in DEV.
attemptLog[].errorCodeFor example twilio_http_400, token_unregistered (a dead push token, now retired), push_rejected (Firebase refused the message; the token is kept: check the configuration), push_unavailable.
callbacks[]The status report Adera posts to the partner's callbackUrl when the message reaches a final status. status: PENDING (will retry), PROCESSING, DELIVERED (the partner answered 2xx), FAILED (gave up after 8 attempts, or the address is not allowed). lastHttpStatus, lastError (unreachable, http_500, scheme_not_https, host_not_resolvable, address_not_public, ...), nextAttemptAt. A failing callback never affects the message itself: it only means the partner did not get its report.

Why did it not arrive? A step-by-step answer

Start with what the partner tells you: their own externalId (or the error code they got), the time, the app.

  1. Was it accepted at all? Look in the log for the app and the time window (GET .../notifications?appId=...&from=...). Not in the log means the request was refused, and the partner got an error. Ask for it. The common ones: ip_not_allowed (fix the addresses), key_revoked_or_expired / invalid_signature / wrong_environment (keys), app_draft / app_paused / app_killed (status), capability_not_granted (capabilities), template_inactive / template_expired / template_not_yet_valid (templates), param_invalid (their values), quota_exceeded (limits: check usage), user_unreachable (the user is deleted, suspended, unknown to that app, or has no contact for the channel). The full list is on the Errors page.
  2. It is in the log: read status. QUEUED or RETRYING for a long time: look at the detail (reason, nextAttemptAt); check that the delivery worker and the provider are healthy (System page). SUPPRESSED / recipient_opted_out: the user muted the app; nothing to fix. FAILED: read attemptLog; twilio_http_400-style codes mean the number or address was rejected. UNREACHABLE: the account is gone or has no phone/email. EXPIRED: a long provider outage.
  3. SENT but the user says nothing arrived. For IN_APP: the inbox entry exists (the user sees it when opening the app); a missing push usually means SKIPPED / no_devices or token_unregistered in the attempts (the user has no valid device registered). For SMS and email: SENT means the provider accepted it; delivery to the handset or mailbox is not tracked, so check the user's number or address and the provider's own console.
  4. It arrived on the wrong channel. The partner never chooses the channel. A DIRECT template goes by SMS when the user has a phone and notify.sms is granted, otherwise by email. A suspended SMS capability quietly moves traffic to email.
  5. The partner says the status report never came. Look at callbacks in the detail: PENDING with lastError: unreachable or http_500 is the partner's server; FAILED with scheme_not_https or address_not_public is a callback address Adera refuses to call (only public https addresses on ports 443 or 8443 are allowed in production).
