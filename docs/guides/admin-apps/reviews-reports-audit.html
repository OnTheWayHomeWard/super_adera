Reviews, reports and audit

Moderate an app's reviews and reports, and read what staff did to it.

Overview

Three things about an app that staff watch over, besides its configuration: what users say about it (reviews and reports) and what staff did to it (the audit log). All of them are under /admin/api/v1; reading is for any staff role (except the audit log: ADMIN and above), changing is ADMIN and above.

Why this existsAn app's rating and reviews are public. If a review is abusive, or a user reports an app as a scam, staff must be able to hide the review or act on the report quickly, and later prove what they did. The audit log is that proof.

What users do (for context)

  • A signed-in user can rate a LIVE app 1 to 5 stars with an optional comment (up to 2000 characters), once per app (sending again edits their review), and delete it: PUT/DELETE /api/v1/apps/{slug}/my-review. Reviews are shown anonymously (no user id) in the catalog.
  • A user can report a LIVE app from the 3-dot menu: POST /api/v1/apps/{slug}/report with a reason (SPAM, SCAM, INAPPROPRIATE, BROKEN, OTHER) and optional details (up to 2000 characters). A second report by the same user while the first is still open returns the first one (no pile-ups).
  • Both are refused (404) while the app is not LIVE. The average rating and count (ratingAvg, ratingCount on the app) are computed from visible reviews only.

Real example: a demo user rated the throwaway app 2 stars and reported it as BROKEN (both removed at the end of the test):

PUT /api/v1/apps/doc-tmp-admin/my-review
{
  "rating": 2,
  "comment": "Doc test review (will be removed)"
}
-> {"id": "8c730183-cc17-4f81-9da7-4d008d3921a3", "rating": 2, "comment": "Doc test review (will be removed)", "createdAt": "2026-10-26T11:07:33.308235237Z", "updatedAt": "2026-10-26T11:07:33.308235237Z"}

Effects, audit

  • The review disappears from (or returns to) the public list, and the app's ratingAvg and ratingCount are recomputed at once. The author is told: their my-review view shows status: HIDDEN with a message, and editing the review does not unhide it (only you can restore it).
  • Audit: review.moderate (target type app_review, target id the review id, meta {"status", "appId"} plus "reason" when one was given). Reversible: set the other status.

Effects, audit

  • Sets status, handledBy (the staff id) and handledAt; reopening (OPEN) clears both again. Nothing else happens automatically: a REVIEWED report does not pause the app. To act on an app, change its status (see the status page), then close the report.
  • Audit: report.handle (target type app_report, target id the report id, meta {"status", "appId"} plus "reason" when one was given).

The audit log

Every change staff make is written, in the same database transaction as the change, to an append-only audit log: who (staff id, email and role), what (action name), on which object, details, from which IP, and when. A change and its audit event are committed together or not at all.

Reads are not audited. Template changes are not audited (see Known issues). Events from other services (for example user status changes) land in the same log.

  • actorEmail is filled from the staff table; it is null for events whose actor is not (or no longer) a staff member.
  • ip is the staff member's address as the gateway saw it.
