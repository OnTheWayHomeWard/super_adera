The fake bridge

The fake window.adera used by the last two suites lives only in bridge-lab/tests/stub-bridge.js. It is never served to the phone, so on the phone a missing bridge always shows as missing.

The backend suite changes Demo Abebe's consents for Bridge Lab only (name and in-app notifications) and leaves two Bridge Lab messages in his inbox. It touches nothing of the demo apps.
