Worked example: Demo Ride

A user's whole journey with a ride-hailing mini app: launch, permissions one at a time, a blocked permission unblocked in the menu, Disconnect, and the callbacks the partner received.

Overview

The whole journey of a user with the seeded mini app Demo Ride (a ride-hailing app). Everything below was captured by the end-to-end test tests/e2e/permissions.py against the running dev stack, through the gateway, with a real seeded user (Demo Kidist, who has a phone), the real partner key of Demo Ride, and a temporary staff account. Ids are shortened where marked. Nothing real was sent: dev mode uses a console SMS, a local mail catcher and a fake push.

  1. Staff granted Demo Ride user.basic, user.full_name, location.fine and notify.sms, approved a purpose sentence for each, and the app is LIVE.
  2. The user taps Demo Ride. No dialog appears. The super app calls launch.
  3. She taps "Find a ride". The mini app asks for location.fine; she allows it.
  4. The mini app's backend verifies the token and reads the profile: only the alias and granted: ["user.basic"], because the name was never requested.
  5. At the end of the trip the mini app wants to text the driver's code. She denies; the partner's SMS is refused. It asks again later with an explanation; she denies again: BLOCKED.
  6. Next day the mini app asks once more and gets blocked with no dialog; it points her to the settings, where she switches SMS on; the next SMS goes through.
  7. She turns location off in the menu; the mini app asks again with an Allow button.
  8. She taps Disconnect: everything becomes DENIED, the partner gets a user.disconnected callback and the next profile call is refused.

  "state": "NOT_ASKED",

    "expiresInSeconds": 300,

7. What the partner's server was told

With a callback address set for Demo Ride's DEV environment, the partner's receiver got these events in order (all signatures verified against the Adera key):

user.consent_changed   location.fine  GRANTED
user.consent_changed   notify.sms     DENIED
user.consent_changed   notify.sms     BLOCKED
user.consent_changed   notify.sms     GRANTED
user.consent_changed   notify.inapp   GRANTED
user.consent_changed   notify.inapp   DENIED
user.consent_changed   notify.inapp   GRANTED
user.consent_changed   user.full_name GRANTED
user.consent_changed   user.full_name DENIED
user.consent_changed   location.fine  DENIED
user.disconnected      (notify.sms, notify.inapp, user.basic) 
user.consent_changed   user.basic     GRANTED
