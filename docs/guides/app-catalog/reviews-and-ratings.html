Reviews and ratings

Rating an app, writing, editing and deleting a review, listing other people's reviews, and what hidden or paused apps do to them.

Overview

Users can rate a mini app from 1 to 5 stars and add a short comment. The average and count are part of every app object; the individual reviews are listed anonymously. These screens use four endpoints: list reviews, get my review, put my review (create/edit) and delete my review.

Rules (verified)

RuleDetail
Who may reviewAny signed-in user, any LIVE app. There is no "must have used the app" check, no purchase check and no market check.
One per user per appEnforced by a database unique key on (app, user). A second PUT edits the existing review.
RatingRequired whole number 1 to 5.
CommentOptional, up to 2000 characters, trimmed; empty is stored as none. Plain text. No title, no photos, no language tag, no reply from the publisher.
EditsAllowed any number of times, no time limit. The review keeps its id and creation time; updatedAt changes. A PUT replaces the whole review (an omitted comment is removed).
DeletionThe author can delete their review any time while the app is LIVE; idempotent.
AnonymityThe public review has no author information at all. Staff can see more.
Order and pagingNewest first by creation date; page from 0, size 1 to 100 (default 20). No total, no sort option, no filter by stars.
Effect on the ratingEvery create, edit, delete and staff hide/restore recomputes ratingAvg (mean of visible ratings, 2 decimals) and ratingCount at once.

What app status does to reviews

App statusList reviewsGet / delete my reviewPut my reviewExisting reviews and the rating
LIVEWorksWorksWorksCounted
PAUSED404 not_foundWorks (author may read and delete their review)404 not_foundKept in the database and still counted in the stored average; invisible to other users until the app is LIVE again. Deleting your review updates the stored average.
KILLED404Works404Kept; invisible to other users.
DRAFT404404404Normally none exist.

Verified on a throwaway app (2026-10-26): after PAUSED, GET .../my-review and DELETE .../my-review answered 200; PUT .../my-review, GET .../reviews, GET /apps/{app} and POST .../report answered 404 not_found. Practical consequence: a user can still see and remove their own review of a paused or killed app but cannot edit it; the catalog, the review list and reporting stay LIVE-only.

Hidden reviews

Staff can set a review to HIDDEN (spam, abuse) and back to VISIBLE. What the user side shows (verified by hiding a review of a throwaway app):

  • The reviews list does not contain it, and it no longer counts in ratingAvg/ratingCount.
  • The author is told. GET my-review and the response of PUT my-review carry "status": "VISIBLE" or "status": "HIDDEN". A hidden review also has a message ("Hidden by moderators: ... Editing it does not restore it."). Verified on a throwaway app.
  • If the author edits it, it stays hidden: an edit does not bring it back (only staff can restore it). The PUT answers 200 with "status": "HIDDEN" and the same message, so the screen can say so.
  • If the author deletes it and writes a new one, the new one is visible.

Example (hidden review, author's view):

{
  "id": "fa668738-7c00-4ea2-85a3-746eed2d2dcf",
  "rating": 4,
  "comment": "Works well.",
  "status": "HIDDEN",
  "message": "Hidden by moderators: it is not shown to other users and does not count in the rating. Editing it does not restore it.",
  "createdAt": "2026-10-26T17:32:44.081901Z",
  "updatedAt": "2026-10-26T17:32:44.129735Z"
}

Show a clear "removed by moderators" state when status is HIDDEN. The public list GET .../reviews never contains hidden reviews and has no status field.

UI recommendations

  • Store page: stars and count from the app; "Rate this app" row (5 tappable stars) that opens a sheet with an optional comment field (2000 character counter). If my-review exists, pre-fill and title it "Edit your review" with a "Delete" action.
  • Only the stars are needed to submit: allow Send with no comment.
  • After Send, show the new average with the count from a fresh detail fetch.
  • Reviews list: stars, text, date. No names or avatars exist, so do not draw placeholders for them.
  • Empty list: "No reviews yet. Be the first." (the count is 0).

Not built yet

  • Review language tag, title, photos, "helpful" votes, replies from the publisher.
  • Reporting a review (reports exist only for apps).
  • A list of "my reviews" across apps, the author's view of moderation status, notification when a review is hidden.
  • A star histogram, sorting (most helpful, highest, lowest), a total count of reviews other than the visible count.
  • A rule limiting reviews to users who actually opened the app (the app launcher does not exist).

Endpoints

GET/api/v1/apps/{idOrSlug}/reviewsList the visible reviews of an app
Who may call
Signed-in end user. Any signed-in user may review any LIVE app: there is no check that they used it, and no market check.
Rate limit
300 requests per 60 s per client IP (route apps-catalog).
Headers
Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN; Content-Type: application/json on PUT.
Implemented in
AppPublicController#reviews, ReviewService#visible

Purpose

Show what other people said, on the store page ("Ratings and reviews"). Reviews are anonymous: there is no author id, name, avatar or language in the response. Only VISIBLE reviews are returned (staff can hide abusive ones). Order is newest first by creation time and cannot be changed.

Request parameters

NameInTypeRequiredConstraintsWhy it exists
idOrSlugpathstringyesApp id or slug; app must be LIVEWhich app
pagequeryintegernoDefault 0 (first page). Negative values are treated as 0. A page past the end returns [].Paging
sizequeryintegernoDefault 20. Clamped silently to 1..100 (0 becomes 1, 999 becomes 100). A non-number gives 400 invalid_request.Page size

Example request

curl -s "$GATEWAY/api/v1/apps/demo-ride/reviews?page=0&size=2" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"

Example response (200)

HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Content-Type: application/json

[
  {
    "id": "013f8df7-ef27-463e-a5d4-2ca4187638b3",
    "rating": 1,
    "comment": "Cancelled twice in a row.",
    "createdAt": "2026-10-25T12:32:45.911326Z",
    "updatedAt": "2026-10-25T12:32:45.911326Z"
  },
  {
    "id": "46139366-a5a0-4b29-89ac-ed1497c157dd",
    "rating": 3,
    "comment": null,
    "createdAt": "2026-10-25T12:32:42.135232Z",
    "updatedAt": "2026-10-25T12:32:42.135232Z"
  }
]

Fields: id (review id), rating (1 to 5), comment (string or null, max 2000 chars), createdAt, updatedAt (they differ when the review was edited). A plain array: no total, no "has more" flag. Keep requesting page+1 until you get fewer than size items (or an empty array).

Errors

StatusError codeWhen it happensWhat the caller should do
404not_foundThe app does not exist or is not LIVE (DRAFT, PAUSED, KILLED).Tell the user the app is not available; remove it from lists.
400invalid_requestpage or size is not an integer.Fix the client; do not retry.
401(empty body)Missing or invalid session.Sign in again.
429(HTML page)Gateway rate limit (300 per 60 s per IP).Back off.

How to use it

  • Show 5 to 10 on the store page with "See all", and load more pages when scrolling.
  • Show the comment as plain text (users write it). A review with a rating and no comment is normal: show stars only.
  • The user's own review appears in this list too (unless hidden). Highlight it by matching id with the id from my-review.
  • Dates: show updatedAt with "edited" when it is later than createdAt.
GET/api/v1/apps/{idOrSlug}/my-reviewGet my own review of an app
Who may call
Signed-in end user. Any signed-in user may review any LIVE app: there is no check that they used it, and no market check.
Rate limit
300 requests per 60 s per client IP (route apps-catalog).
Headers
Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN; Content-Type: application/json on PUT.
Implemented in
AppPublicController#myReview, ReviewService#mine

Purpose

Returns the signed-in user's own review so the screen can pre-fill the stars and text ("Edit your review") or show "Rate this app" when there is none.

Example request

curl -s "$GATEWAY/api/v1/apps/doc-tmp-store/my-review" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"

404not_foundTwo different causes with the same body: the app is unknown or a DRAFT (PAUSED and KILLED apps still answer), or the user has not reviewed it.Treat 404 on this call as "no review" when you know the app is LIVE (you just loaded it); otherwise reload the app.

How to use it

  • Call it when the store page opens, in parallel with the reviews list. Expect 404 as the normal "not reviewed" answer, not as an error to show.
  • The response has no status field: if staff hid the review it is still returned here as if it were public. See Hidden reviews.
PUT/api/v1/apps/{idOrSlug}/my-reviewCreate or edit my review
Who may call
Signed-in end user. Any signed-in user may review any LIVE app: there is no check that they used it, and no market check.
Rate limit
300 requests per 60 s per client IP (route apps-catalog).
Headers
Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN; Content-Type: application/json on PUT.
Implemented in
AppPublicController#review, ReviewService#upsert

Purpose

One call both creates the review and edits it. Each user has at most one review per app (a database rule): the first PUT creates it, every later PUT replaces it. The app's average rating and count are recomputed immediately.

Request body (JSON object)

NameTypeRequiredConstraintsWhy it exists
ratingintegeryesWhole number 1 to 5. Strings ("5"), decimals (4.5), 0 and 6 are rejected.The star rating that feeds ratingAvg.
commentstring or nullnoUp to 2000 characters after trimming. Empty or blank, or null, stores no comment.Optional free text.

Any other key is rejected with "unknown field": there is no language, title or lang field. Reviews are not tagged with a language.

Replace, not merge

A PUT replaces the review. If you edit only the stars and leave comment out, the old comment is deleted. Always send the full state (re-send the existing comment when the user only changes the stars). Verified: PUT {"rating":4,"comment":"Works well."} then PUT {"rating":5} returns "comment": null.

Example request

curl -s -X PUT "$GATEWAY/api/v1/apps/doc-tmp-store/my-review" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"rating": 4, "comment": "Works well."}'

Example response (200)

HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "id": "446fcaed-807a-4dce-9a7e-e9fc4c7cb534",
  "rating": 4,
  "comment": "Works well.",
  "status": "VISIBLE",
  "createdAt": "2026-10-26T10:51:32.249809178Z",
  "updatedAt": "2026-10-26T10:51:32.249809178Z"
}

Create and edit both answer 200 (not 201). The id stays the same across edits; createdAt stays, updatedAt moves. (Note the PUT response can show more fractional digits than a later GET of the same value; compare times, not strings.)

Example: validation errors

curl -s -X PUT "$GATEWAY/api/v1/apps/doc-tmp-store/my-review" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"rating": 6}'
HTTP/1.1 400 Bad Request

{
  "error": "validation_failed",
  "fields": {
    "rating": "must be a whole number from 1 to 5"
  }
}
# {"rating": 3, "comment": "<2001 characters>", "lang": "en"}
HTTP/1.1 400 Bad Request

{
  "error": "validation_failed",
  "fields": {
    "lang": "unknown field",
    "comment": "max 2000 characters"
  }
}

Errors

StatusError codeWhen it happensWhat the caller should do
400validation_failedBad rating (missing, not an integer, outside 1..5), comment longer than 2000, or an unknown key. fields names each problem.Show the message next to the field; disable Send until valid.
400invalid_requestBody is not valid JSON or not a JSON object (for example [1]).Client bug.
415unsupported_media_typeNo Content-Type: application/json.Client bug.
404not_foundThe app does not exist or is not LIVE (DRAFT, PAUSED, KILLED).Tell the user the app is not available; remove it from lists.
409conflictVery rare: two requests from the same user created the first review at the same instant and hit the one-review-per-user rule.Repeat the PUT once (it will now edit).
401(empty body)Missing or invalid session.Sign in again.
429(HTML page)Gateway rate limit (300 per 60 s per IP).Back off.

How to use it

  • Update the screen from the returned review, then refresh the app detail (or recompute locally) to get the new ratingAvg/ratingCount.
  • Make the Send button idempotent in the UI: PUT is safe to repeat.
  • Trim whitespace client-side the way the server does; a comment of only spaces is stored as none.
DELETE/api/v1/apps/{idOrSlug}/my-reviewDelete my review
Who may call
Signed-in end user. Any signed-in user may review any LIVE app: there is no check that they used it, and no market check.
Rate limit
300 requests per 60 s per client IP (route apps-catalog).
Headers
Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN; Content-Type: application/json on PUT.
Implemented in
AppPublicController#deleteReview, ReviewService#delete

Purpose

Removes the user's review and recomputes the app's rating. Use it for "Delete my review". It is idempotent: deleting when there is no review also answers 200.

Example request and response

curl -s -X DELETE "$GATEWAY/api/v1/apps/doc-tmp-store/my-review" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Content-Type: application/json

{"status":"deleted"}

The same body comes back on a second call (verified), so you cannot tell "deleted now" from "was not there".

Errors

StatusError codeWhen it happensWhat the caller should do
404not_foundThe app does not exist or was never published (DRAFT). PAUSED and KILLED apps still answer 200 here.Tell the user the app is not available; remove it from lists.
401(empty body)Missing or invalid session.Sign in again.
429(HTML page)Gateway rate limit (300 per 60 s per IP).Back off.

How to use it

  • Ask for confirmation first; there is no undo.
  • After success clear the local copy of the review and refresh the rating.
  • If the app is PAUSED or KILLED, the user cannot delete their review (404). See below.
