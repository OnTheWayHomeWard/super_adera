Moderation: reviews

See every review across all mini apps, hide or restore reviews, and understand what that does to ratings and to what users see.

Overview

Users rate a mini app from 1 to 5 stars and may add a comment of up to 2000 characters (one review per user per app; sending again edits it). Reviews are published immediately: there is no approval queue. Moderation means finding the reviews that break the rules and hiding them. A hidden review stays in the database and in the staff list, but users no longer see it and it stops counting in the app's rating. Hiding is reversible (restore). In the backoffice this is the screen Moderation, tab Reviews (and the tab Reviews of an app).

Why hide instead of delete

Staff keep the evidence (who wrote what, when) and can undo a mistake. The audit log records who hid which review and when.

List reviews across all apps

GET/admin/api/v1/reviewsEvery review of every app, newest first, hidden ones included
Why
One queue for moderators instead of opening each app. Filter on status HIDDEN to audit what was hidden, or on one app to read its feedback.
Auth
Staff aal2 Bearer, any role (read). Changing needs ADMIN.
Rate limit
300/min/IP
Backoffice
Moderation > Reviews (columns When, App, Rating, Comment, User, Status; buttons Hide / Restore for admins)

Use in a client

Read X-Total-Count for pagination. Show hidden rows greyed out with a Restore button. Do not rely on the comment being plain text: render it escaped.

Hide or restore a review

PATCH/admin/api/v1/reviews/{reviewId}Hide (HIDDEN) or restore (VISIBLE) a review
Why
Remove abusive, spam or off-topic reviews from public view, or put back one that was hidden by mistake.
Auth
Staff aal2 Bearer, role ADMIN or SUPER_ADMIN (VIEWER: 403 insufficient_role)
Audit action
review.moderate (target type app_review, meta {status, appId})
Reversible
Yes, any number of times
Backoffice
Moderation > Reviews: Hide (asks for confirmation) and Restore

Body

FieldTypeRequiredConstraintsWhy it exists
statusstringyesVISIBLE or HIDDEN (any case)The new state.
No reason field

The endpoint accepts no reason: other fields in the body are ignored and nothing but the new status is stored. Write the reason in your ticket and quote the review id; the audit log shows who and when, not why.

Request: hide

curl -sS -X PATCH "$GATEWAY/admin/api/v1/reviews/$REVIEW_ID" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"status":"HIDDEN"}'

Side effects

The app's stored rating is recalculated in the same step (see below). One audit row. Nothing is sent to the author or to the partner.

Effect on ratings and on what people see

Verified on a throwaway app with one 1-star review (the app was LIVE; the app detail endpoint shows ratingAvg and ratingCount):

StateratingAvgratingCountPublic list GET /api/v1/apps/{id}/reviews
Review VISIBLE1.01contains the review
After HIDDEN0.00[] (empty)
After VISIBLE again1.01contains the review
  • The rating is ROUND(AVG(rating), 2) over VISIBLE reviews only, 0 when there are none; the count is the number of VISIBLE reviews. Both are recalculated on every review change by users and by staff.
  • Users never see hidden reviews or their status. The author's own my-review endpoint still returns a hidden review (without its status), so the author is not told it was hidden.
  • Suspending or deleting the author does not hide their reviews. Hide them yourself. (The seeded review "This service is garbage and the owners are idiots." on demo-food belongs to the deleted demo user and is still VISIBLE.)
  • The review text is stored as typed; only staff and the author see a hidden one.

Worked scenario

A user writes "BUY FOLLOWERS CHEAP, DM me on telegram" as a review of demo-food. In Moderation > Reviews you filter by app, see it, press Hide: the app's average no longer includes that 1-star. You then open the author (the userId link) and decide whether to suspend them. The sample data already contains this pair (HIDDEN review by the "Demo Suspended" user).

Not built yet

  • No reason or note when hiding, no moderator notes.
  • No automatic spam detection and no pre-moderation queue (reviews appear immediately).
  • No notification to the author when a review is hidden.
  • No way to delete a review from the staff side (users can delete their own).
