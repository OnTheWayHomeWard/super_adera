Notification visibility

Read-only, cross-app visibility of notification delivery for support: list, detail with attempts, statistics, usage, and a glossary of statuses and reasons.

Overview

Mini apps (partners) send notifications to Adera users through the partner API: an SMS, an email, or an in-app message with a push. Staff can see what was sent, to which app's account, with which outcome, across all apps, without seeing the message text or who received it. This is how support answers "why did the user not get my message?" and how the owner spots a partner that is misbehaving. The endpoints belong to notification-service (gateway route admin-notification), are read-only and open to every staff role. Backoffice: Notifications (list with stats header, detail page with an explanation box) and the tab Notification log of an app. Templates and per-app limits are configured in the mini-app administration guide.

Why message values are hidden

Message values and recipients can contain personal data (names, codes, amounts). Staff diagnose delivery from status, channel, reason and attempts only.

Use in a client

There is no filter by recipient: the user is deliberately not exposed. To investigate one person, work from the app, the time window and the partner's externalId.

Fields

FieldMeaning
status, reasonOutcome and its cause (glossaries below). reason is null while things are fine.
attemptsDelivery attempts made.
templateId, templateVersionWhich template and version rendered the message.

attemptLog[]Each try: channel, provider (for example mailpit in DEV, or sandbox), outcome, errorCode, at. Empty when the decision was made before any provider was called (muted, unreachable user).
callbacks[]The status callbacks to the partner's callbackUrl: status (PENDING, PROCESSING, DELIVERED or FAILED), attempts, lastHttpStatus, lastError, nextAttemptAt.

Statuses glossary

StatusMeaningWhat staff do
QUEUEDAccepted and waiting for the delivery worker.Nothing; wait seconds.
PROCESSINGA worker is sending it now.Nothing.
RETRYINGAn attempt failed for a temporary reason; it will be tried again until it expires.Check attemptLog errorCode and the provider.
SENTHanded to the provider (SMS gateway, email server, push) successfully. For in-app messages: stored in the user's inbox and pushed to their devices. The handset's delivery receipt is not tracked.If the user still says nothing arrived, check the user's number and mutes.
FAILEDPermanent failure (bad number or address) or too many attempts. Not retried.Read reason (max_attempts:<code> or the provider's code).
UNREACHABLENo way to reach the user: deleted or suspended account, or no phone (SMS) or email on file.Look the user up (Users).
SUPPRESSEDDeliberately not sent: the user muted this app's channel, or the recipient is not on the live allowlist.Normal; nothing to fix.
EXPIREDIt kept failing until its deadline passed.Check the provider health on the System page.

Final states: SENT, FAILED, UNREACHABLE, SUPPRESSED, EXPIRED. Non-final: QUEUED, PROCESSING, RETRYING.

Reasons and attempt outcomes

reasonUsually with statusMeaning
recipient_opted_outSUPPRESSEDThe user muted this channel for this app (three-dot menu of the app window).
user_unreachableUNREACHABLEThe user is deleted or suspended (or no usable channel). At acceptance the partner gets 422 user_unreachable; if it happens after acceptance (a race) the notification ends with this reason.
no_phone / no_emailUNREACHABLEThe user has no phone (SMS) or no email on file.
not_allowlistedSUPPRESSEDLive providers are restricted to an allowlist in this deployment and the recipient is not on it, so nothing real was sent.
max_attempts:<code>FAILEDEvery attempt failed; the part after the colon is the last error.
<provider error code>FAILED / EXPIREDFor example mailpit_unreachable (DEV email inbox down): the last transient error when the deadline passed.
expiredEXPIREDThe deadline passed with no more specific reason.
user_directory_unavailableRETRYING / EXPIREDuser-service could not be reached to resolve the recipient.

attemptLog[].outcome is one of OK, SKIPPED (with not_allowlisted or no_devices), TRANSIENT (will retry) or PERMANENT (will not; for example token_unregistered for a dead push token).

What is never shown

  • Message values, rendered text, the partner's data payload.
  • The user's id, alias, phone or email.
  • Partner keys, signatures or callback payloads. Only the callback's status, attempts and last HTTP status are visible.

Errors

400 invalid_request for a malformed uuid (appId, path id), 400 validation_failed for bad from/to, 404 not_found for an unknown notification id. There is no role error: all four endpoints are open to every staff role. See also the error codes page, which includes the partner-facing codes you will meet in support questions.

Not built yet

  • No search by recipient or user, no re-send or cancel button for a notification.
  • No export, no delivery receipts from handsets.
  • No staff-visible log of the one-time-code SMS and emails used for sign-in (they live in the notification database table send_log, see the runbook).
