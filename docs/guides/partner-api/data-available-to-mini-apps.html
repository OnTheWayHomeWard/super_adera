What data does a mini app get from Adera today

Short answer: only what it created itself and the delivery status of it. Nothing about the user: no profile, no contact details, no location, no payments. The mechanism that would give a mini app such data, the frontend bridge with per-user consent, is not built yet.

Summary

Do not plan around data that is not there

A mini-app backend cannot ask Adera who a user is. Adera has no endpoint, for a partner, that returns a user's name, phone number, email address, address, language, avatar, location, payment information or device. The only user-related value a partner holds is the pseudonymous id u_p_..., and even obtaining that is not possible by the partner itself today (User ids).

How this was verified

  • Gateway: the only route that serves partner traffic is /v1/* (plus /.well-known/jwks.json). The notification service implements under /v1/ only GET /templates, GET /templates/{id}, POST /notifications and GET /notifications/{externalId}.
  • Probing: with a valid signed key, GET /v1/users/{id}, /v1/users, /v1/me, /v1/profile, /v1/location, /v1/payments, /v1/apps, /v1/capabilities and /v1/session all answered 404 Not Found.
  • Capabilities: the catalog of capabilities staff can grant has ten entries (table below). Only the three notify.* ones change what the API does. The other seven are stored and shown in the dashboard, but no service reads them. Their purpose is the future consent sheet shown to users.
  • Frontend bridge: no code path hands a mini app a user id or data. The registry stores an entryUrl and allowedOrigins per app "for the frontend bridge", validated but not enforced or used.
  • Internal endpoints that carry user data (/internal/users/{id}/delivery-context, /internal/aliases) are protected by an internal secret and are not routed by the gateway (404 through it); they serve Adera's own services only.

What this means for a mini-app design today

  • Your mini app must have its own user accounts and its own source of contact data. Adera cannot supply or verify them.
  • You cannot yet link "this person in my system" to "this person in Adera". Until the bridge exists, the only users you can notify are ones whose u_p_... ids Adera staff handed to you (test users), plus synthetic u_p_test_* ids in DEV. Real production notification traffic depends on the bridge.
  • Do not design features that need the user's phone, name or location from Adera. Ask the user in your own screens.
  • You get no data back about what the user did with a notification.

Not built yet

  • The frontend bridge: the Adera app opens your mini app, gets the user's consent per capability, and hands over the pseudonymous id and an access mechanism.
  • Any endpoint returning user.basic, user.full_name, user.contact, user.address, location.*.
  • Payments: pay.request, payment callbacks.
  • A partner endpoint to list or search the notifications it sent.
  • Per-user consent records and the consent sheet.
