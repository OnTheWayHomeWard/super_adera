Error codes

Every answer of the banner list that is not a list.

StatuserrorWhenWhat to do
400validation_failedfields.placement or fields.category is not one of the values, or fields.limit is not 1 to 50.Fix the call; it is a bug in the app.
429(gateway page)More than 300 calls a minute from one address.Hide the banners and try again later.
5xx(any)The service is down or restarting.Hide the placement; never block the screen.
