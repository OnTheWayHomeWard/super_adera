Rendering each placement

What each placement is for, which image size to load, and the empty and error states.

Placements

PlacementWhereSuggested layoutSuggested limit
HOME_HEROTop of the home screenFull-width carousel, one banner at a time, auto-advancing; title and subtitle over the bottom of the picture.5
FEATURED"Featured" strip on the home screenHorizontal row of cards about 70% of the screen wide, title under the picture, the linked app's logo and name if app is present.10
PROMOTIONSPromotions / offers screenVertical list of full-width cards with title and subtitle.20
CATALOG_TOPTop of the mini-app catalogOne slim full-width banner (show the first one only, or a small carousel).3

The backoffice preview uses these layouts, so what staff see while editing is close to what users see.

Choosing the image size

Pick the smallest size whose width covers the space the banner takes on screen, in physical pixels (logical width times the device pixel ratio). Example: a full-width hero on a 393-point-wide phone at 3x needs 1179 px, so large; a 70% card on the same phone needs 825 px, so medium; a list thumbnail takes small. Use width and height to keep the aspect ratio and to reserve the space before the picture arrives, so the screen does not jump.

Images are never enlarged by the server, so for a small upload two or three sizes may be the same picture. Staff are told to upload at least 1600 px wide pictures.

Text and languages

  • Show title[lang], falling back to title.en. Same for subtitle; if it has no text, show only the title.
  • Titles are at most 80 characters and subtitles 160. Allow two lines for the title and two for the subtitle, then cut with an ellipsis.
  • Text over a picture needs a dark gradient behind it to stay readable whatever the picture is.

Empty and error states

  • Empty list: hide the placement completely (no empty carousel, no placeholder).
  • Error or no network: hide the placement, or keep showing the last list you had if it is less than an hour old (the image links expire after 60 minutes). Never block the screen on banners.
  • A picture that fails to load: skip that banner.
