Fetching banners

One call per screen: the banners that are showing now for one placement.

Overview

The server filters everything that must not be shown (drafts, paused banners, banners that have not started or have ended, banners of a mini app that is not live) and sorts the rest. The app shows the list it gets, in that order.

GET/api/v1/bannersBanners showing right now
Who may call
Anyone; no sign-in needed
Rate limit
300 per minute per client address (the gateway answers 429 above that)
Caching
The answer carries Cache-Control: public, max-age=60
QueryTypeRequiredMeaning
placementHOME_HERO | FEATURED | PROMOTIONS | CATALOG_TOPnoOnly banners for this place. A screen normally passes its own placement. Without it, banners of all placements come back.
categoryPROMOTION | FEATURED_APP | ANNOUNCEMENT | EVENTnoOnly this category (for example a "Featured apps" strip asks for FEATURED_APP).
limitwhole number 1 to 50no, default 10At most this many, highest priority first.

Request

curl -sS "$GATEWAY/api/v1/banners?placement=HOME_HERO&limit=5"

The answer, field by field

FieldTypeUse
iduuidKey for lists and for analytics events ("banner shown", "banner tapped").
title, subtitlelanguage objectsText over or under the picture. subtitle may be {}.
placement, categoryenumsAlready filtered by your query; the category can drive a small label ("Event", "New app").
actionType, targetenum, string or nullWhat a tap does: see "Handling a tap".
appobject or nullThe linked mini app: id, slug, name, logoUrl. Always present for MINI_APP and FEATURED_APP; may be present for others.
imagesobjectsmall, medium, large, each with a signed url and the pixel width and height.
prioritynumberAlready applied to the order; you do not need to sort.
startsAt, endsAttime, time or nullFor information (for example a countdown on an event banner). Do not hide or show by these yourself.

Caching and refreshing

  • Fetch when the screen opens and when it comes back to the foreground after more than a minute. The 60-second cache header lets HTTP caches reuse the answer.
  • Image links are signed and expire after 60 minutes. Do not store them: fetch the list again and use the new links. Cache the downloaded picture under the banner id plus the image file name (the part of the URL path before ?), which changes whenever staff replace the image.
  • Banners start and end on the server's clock. A banner that ends while the screen is open stays until the next fetch; that is fine.
